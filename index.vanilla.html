        How the dashboard calculates KPIs

        Transparent definitions for consistent reporting.

        Completion rateCompleted tasks ÷ all tasks × 100
        Hours loggedSum of actual hours
        Output volumeSum of output counts
        CTRClicks ÷ impressions × 100
        CPCAd spend ÷ clicks
        Engagement rate (impression-based)Engagements ÷ impressions × 100
        Lead conversion rateConversions ÷ leads × 100

        Use the same metric definitions across platforms when comparing results. Platform-reported reach and engagement definitions may differ.
